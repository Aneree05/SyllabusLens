### PART A (Answer All Questions - 5 Marks Each)

(b) Explain Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF). Formulate the mathematical equation for IDF and explain why it downweights ubiquitous words across documents. [5 Marks]

2. (a) What is Cosine Similarity? Explain why cosine distance is invariant to document length compared to standard Euclidean distance in vector space retrieval. [5 Marks]

(b) Differentiate between Stemming and Lemmatization. Illustrate with examples how the Porter Stemmer differs from dictionary-based morphological analysis. [5 Marks]
